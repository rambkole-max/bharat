Before you visit

The questions we are asked most. For anything else, call or message us.

Ask on WhatsApp
Do you serve both veg and non-veg?

Yes. There’s a full vegetarian menu alongside the non-veg one. Tell us your preference when you book.

Is it good for kids and families?

Very. Children are looked after, there are private rooms for big families, and nobody is rushed.

Can you set up a birthday or anniversary?

Yes. Candlelight can be set up in a private dining room. Just mention it when you plan your table.

Is there a bar?

Yes, a relaxed, separate bar with a live-music corner and fair prices.

Can you host weddings and corporate meetings?

Yes. We have two banquet halls, Samiksha and Sanskruti, and AV-ready board rooms for meetings and offsites. Use the event planner.

Is parking available?

Yes, on-site parking at the venue in Latur MIDC.

How do I book a table?

Tap “Plan my table”, choose who is coming and when, and send the request on WhatsApp. Your table is held once we reply to confirm.

Planning something bigger?

Weddings, receptions, birthdays and offsites. Tell us the day and the guest count and see which hall or room fits.
